## Product Overview

**GMaps B2B URL Extractor** is a production-ready Google Chrome Extension (built on **Manifest V3**) engineered to streamline and accelerate the collection of clean, structured business URLs and geographic lead data directly from Google Maps.

Designed for **SMEs, marketing agencies, sales teams, and B2B prospecting specialists**, it operates as a zero-friction data capture layer. In active production environments, it works in direct synergy with **[b2b-lead-scraper-etl](https://github.com/pratico/b2b-lead-scraper-etl)**, feeding clean target URLs straight into automated DOM enrichment, company size qualification, and outreach pipelines—with zero manual copy-pasting.

## The Problem Solved

Teams relying on Google Maps for B2B lead generation, local market audits, or point-of-interest indexing face a recurring operational bottleneck: **manually copying and cleaning business URLs and listing details is slow, repetitive, and error-prone**.

The concrete business impact includes:

- **Hours lost every week** manually copying, trimming, and formatting long tracking URLs from dozens or hundreds of business listings.
- **Transcription errors & duplicates** that degrade CRM data quality and disrupt downstream outreach campaigns.
- **Pipeline bottlenecks:** Slow manual data collection starves downstream enrichment and sales workflows.

**GMaps B2B URL Extractor** eliminates this friction completely: one click captures a normalized, standardized URL ready for immediate ingestion.

## Operator Workflow

The workflow requires zero technical setup and fits seamlessly into daily browser operations:

1. **Navigation:** The operator searches for target businesses, niches, or geographic areas on Google Maps in Chrome as usual.
2. **One-Click Activation:** Clicking the extension icon intercepts the active Google Maps business listing in real time.
3. **URL Normalization & Extraction:** The extension strips tracking noise and extracts the clean, canonical listing URL.
4. **Pipeline Ingestion:** The clean output is immediately available to copy, export, or feed directly into **[b2b-lead-scraper-etl](https://github.com/pratico/b2b-lead-scraper-etl)**, CRMs, or spreadsheets.

The end result: **turning minutes of repetitive manual work per batch into seconds**, with 100% consistent formatting across every record.

## Key Features & Operational Benefits (ROI)

- ⚡ **One-Click Extraction:** Zero learning curve or training required; any team member can operate it immediately.
- 🎯 **Clean, Standardized Output:** Strips noisy query parameters so every extracted URL is ready for automated ETL ingestion.
- 🔗 **Seamless Ecosystem Integration:** Actively used in production alongside **[b2b-lead-scraper-etl](https://github.com/pratico/b2b-lead-scraper-etl)** to bridge manual discovery with automated website scraping and lead qualification.
- ⏱️ **Measurable Time Savings (Immediate ROI):** Reduces per-listing capture time from minutes to seconds across high-volume prospecting sessions.
- 🛡️ **Data Integrity:** Eliminates human copy-paste mistakes and broken links in commercial databases.

## High-Level Architecture & Stack

Built on modern, lightweight web standards to guarantee browser stability, low memory footprint, and full compliance with Chrome's latest extension security policies:

- **Chrome Extension Manifest V3 (MV3):** Future-proof architecture utilizing service workers and strict permission scoping for native browser performance.
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+):** Fast, zero-overhead DOM and URL parsing without heavy runtime dependencies.
- **Bootstrap 5:** Clean, responsive popup interface designed for rapid operator interaction.
